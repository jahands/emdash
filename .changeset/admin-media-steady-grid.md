Fixes media cards jumping when you upload or search in the media library.

New uploads now appear after the cards already on screen, in the order you uploaded them, and the library scrolls to the newest one if it's out of view. The library goes back to newest-first order when you change the page, search, type filter or folder. Search results replace the grid at once instead of moving the cards that were already showing.
